# Kovemu MVP Navigation & Discovery Direction

> **Status:** Agreed MVP Direction

## Core Principle

Kovemu is **not a search site**.

Kovemu is a **Discovery Platform**.

Search is a feature. Discovery is the product.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Navigation is **service navigation**, not in-page scrolling.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Discover

The core experience of Kovemu.

-   Pinterest-inspired infinite feed
-   Personalized discovery
-   Mixed creator categories
-   Endless exploration

Goal:

> Help users discover creators they didn't know before.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Categories

Initial MVP:

-   Music
-   Dance
-   Film
-   Art
-   Cosplay

Each category has its own dedicated page.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Rankings

Dedicated ranking page.

Submenus:

-   Trending
-   Popular
-   Rising

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## New

Dedicated page introducing newly joined creators.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## UX Direction

Inspired by **Naver Webtoon** navigation.

Navigation should open dedicated experiences rather than scroll to
sections.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Home

Home acts as a curated landing page.

1.  Header
2.  Hero
3.  Navigation
4.  Curated creator sections

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Long-term Vision

Combine:

-   Korean usability
-   Western minimalism
-   Pinterest discovery
-   Steam exploration

The goal is to make discovering Korean creators enjoyable.
